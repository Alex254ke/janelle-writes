The workflow

Five calm, accountable stages.

The order remains visible to the people involved, with a clear next action at every stage.

1

Create a complete brief

The employer or student adds the subject, deadline, instructions, referencing style, budget, and supporting files.

2

Select the right writer

Writers review available work and place bids, or an employer assigns a preferred writer directly.

3

Work and communicate

Messages, progress updates, questions, and additional materials stay attached to the relevant order.

4

Submit for review

The writer uploads the completed work. The order stays in review until the owner approves it, requests a revision, or opens a dispute.

5

Approve, pay, and review

After approval, payment and platform fees are recorded clearly, and both sides can leave useful feedback.

Protection by design

Important decisions stay visible.

A

Submission is not completion

A submitted order remains under review until the employer or student accepts the work.

B

Revisions remain organized

Requested changes and replacement files stay connected to the order history.

C

Support is reachable

Users can contact support when a task, payment, or account issue needs assistance.
